Fuse

Servers stop asking “who is this?” and ask “has this client used up its budget?”

A deposit backs one credential. It buys a few anonymous first contacts a day, anywhere on the web, and each site sees a pseudonym no other site can link. Spend the same slot twice and the two points give away the key, so the credential burns.
